OVERVIEW

Socria is a Human-First AI company built around a simple belief:

People should think for themselves.

AI should strengthen human thinking, not replace it.

While many AI products focus on generating answers, Socria focuses on helping users arrive at their own understanding through reflection, reasoning, and dialogue.

The brand exists at the intersection of philosophy, education, technology, and personal growth.

---

SLOGAN

Think For Yourself.

This is the foundation of the Socria brand.

It is both a statement and a challenge.

As artificial intelligence becomes increasingly capable of generating answers, content, and decisions, Socria encourages people to remain active participants in their own thinking.

Every product decision, feature, conversation, and experience should reinforce this idea.

---

MISSION

To build AI that helps people think for themselves.

---

VISION

A future where artificial intelligence amplifies human judgment, creativity, and reasoning instead of automating them away.

---

CORE BELIEFS

* Human thought should remain central.
* Questions are often more valuable than answers.
* Understanding matters more than output.
* Reflection creates better decisions.
* AI should be a thinking partner, not a replacement.
* Independent thinking is a competitive advantage.
