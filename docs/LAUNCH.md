## Show HN — the physics surfaces

Hacker News rewards something you can use immediately, and a technical story
told plainly. Post Tuesday–Thursday, mid-morning US Eastern. Stay in the
thread for the first two hours and answer everything.

**Title** (80 characters max):

> Show HN: Socria – a Kerr black hole you can steer, inside an AI thinking tool

**URL:**

> https://socria.app/surfaces?utm_source=hackernews&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=show-hn

**First comment:**

> Hi HN — I'm building Socria, an AI tool whose whole point is the opposite of
> "ask and copy": you talk a problem through and it draws your reasoning
> beside the conversation — claims, assumptions, tensions, open questions —
> so you can see what your view actually rests on. Our line is "think for
> yourself."
>
> The page linked above is one corner of it. When a conversation turns to
> physics, Logos can open a working surface instead of describing one: a Kerr
> black hole with integrated geodesics and a Doppler-coloured disc, the
> thermal history of the universe, and gravity as an N-body integration.
> Every control moves the physics rather than an animation of it.
>
> The rest of the product is the same idea applied to ordinary thinking: a
> map of your argument, models it can actually compute, and documents it
> reads with every citation checked against the page it came from.
>
> You can try it without an account: https://socria.app/chat?utm_source=hackernews&utm_medium=comment&utm_campaign=show-hn
>
> I'd love to hear where it gets the physics wrong, and where the map gets
> your reasoning wrong.

## Product Hunt — Logos 3.5 reads your documents

Schedule for 12:01 a.m. Pacific. Line up the gallery first: the map beside a
conversation, a document with its checked citations, the preview card.

**Name:** Socria

**Tagline** (60 characters max):

> Think for yourself — AI that sharpens your reasoning

**Description** (260 characters max):

> Talk it through and Socria draws your reasoning as you go — what you're
> assuming, what's missing, what it all rests on. Attach a PDF, Word file,
> slides or a spreadsheet and every citation is checked against the page.
> Free to start, no account needed.

**Topics:** Productivity · Artificial Intelligence · Education

**Link:**

> https://socria.app/?utm_source=producthunt&utm_medium=launch&utm_campaign=logos-3-5

**Maker's first comment:**

> Most AI tools are built to think for you. Socria is built to make you
> think better: it asks as well as answers, and Logos draws the shape of your
> reasoning beside the conversation so you can see what you're claiming,
> assuming and still unsure about.
>
> What's new in Logos 3.5: it reads the documents you give it — a PDF, a
> Word file, slides or a spreadsheet, scanned pages included. Ask about one
> and the reply cites the passage it rests on; each citation is checked
> against the document and marked when it is a reading of the passage rather
> than its words. You can open the document beside your map, correct a
> passage that was read wrongly, or turn one of its tables into a chart.
>
> Core works without an account. Logos needs a free one. Socria One
> ($15/month) takes the daily count off.
