# Engagement reading — offline run

Recorded 14 November 2026 with `node evals/engagement/run.mjs`. The model here is a recorder that streams our scripted replies: this shows what the product decides and what the reply model is told, in both arms. It does not show what a model would write, or what people would do.

45 turns of 12 scenarios, through the real route, with the reading off and on.
